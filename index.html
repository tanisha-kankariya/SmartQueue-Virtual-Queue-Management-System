How SmartQueue Works

Designed so anyone can join a queue in under 20 seconds—no app download required.

01
Select Center & Service

Pick your hospital, bank, campus office, or citizen center and view real-time queue load before booking.

02
Book Digital Token

Enter your name, mobile number, and date to receive an instant unique token number like OPD-108.

03
Track Queue Live

Watch how many people are ahead of you, see who's serving now, and check dynamic wait-time updates.

04
Arrive Right On Time

Walk directly to your assigned counter when called by staff—no wasted hours standing in line.

Platform Capabilities

Everything Needed for Smarter Queues

Launch Interactive Demo
Instant Digital Token Engine

Generates collision-free department tokens (e.g., OPD-108, ACC-303) with printable digital confirmation tickets.

Live Queue Progress Radar

Visual stepper, completion progress bar, and live lineup view showing exactly who is being served right now.

Dynamic Wait Estimation

Wait times automatically recalculate based on department service speed and remaining active tokens ahead.

Dedicated Staff Console

One-click Call Next Token, Mark Completed, Skip No-Shows, and Recall Skipped Customers across counters.

Admin Analytics & Controls

Interactive SVG performance charts, daily token volume metrics, plus instant counter & department management.

Real-Time Cross-Tab Sync

Open Staff Console in one tab and Live Tracking in another—every counter action reflects immediately.
